# Abstract :

Lab researchers have demonstrated that a small cluster of human brain cells on a silicon chip can play Pong, proving that merging biology with computing could completely revolutionize artificial intelligence. Today, supercomputers and AI still rely on traditional silicon chips, but Moore's Law is reaching its physical limits as transistors become just one atom thick. At the same time, running modern AI models consumes massive amounts of energy—a supercomputer requires up to 40 megawatts, whereas a biological brain runs on just 20 watts and learns complex environments with far less data. Unlike silicon chips that process tasks sequentially, living cells dynamically intertwine hardware and software to process information fluidly, making biological computing a game-changing alternative even though the technology is still in its infancy.
